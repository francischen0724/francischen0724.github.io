Xiwen Chen

ML Researcher & Engineer USC M.S. in Computer Science (AI) · Los Angeles, CA · Open to Relocate

I build AI systems that make better use of limited data and computation, turning research ideas into reliable software. My work spans agentic AI, data-centric ML, computer vision, and production engineering. One question connects much of my work:

Which data is most useful, and when is expensive computation actually worth it?

At USC, advised by Prof. Ruishan Liu, I led two research directions. RadOncGym explores when agents should use costly evaluations, reducing full evaluations by 81% while retaining 95% of optimization gains. TAVO and TAC focus on data selection and generalization under distribution shift, with TAVO outperforming the strongest fixed-selection baseline using just 12% of source data. My work on RadOncGym and TAVO resulted in two first-author papers accepted at WACV 2027.

At EGUOO Wellness, I owned the end-to-end development and AWS deployment of an AI wellness platform with a stateful LangGraph agent. A 1,000-participant A/B test increased completion from 43% to 78% and reduced average completion time by 45%. Across 320 pilot recommendations, the agent achieved 95% constraint validity, 2.2 s P95 latency, and 35% lower inference cost per successful task than a stateless LLM.

Previously, I applied data-curation ideas to speech ML pipelines at 2 Cube Global and worked on distributed-storage performance and reliability across a 60-node Linux environment at Xiaomi.

Actively seeking AI/ML, software engineering, and applied research roles.
