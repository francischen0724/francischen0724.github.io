TAVO studies target-aware source-data valuation for cross-domain medical image segmentation. The project combines multiple feature- and gradient-based valuation signals through surrogate-guided optimization to select compact training subsets under fixed training budgets.

The goal is to reduce source-data usage while improving target-domain adaptation under label scarcity and distribution shift.
