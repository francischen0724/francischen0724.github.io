TAVO studies target-aware source-data valuation for cross-domain medical image segmentation. It is motivated by a common failure mode in medical imaging: source datasets can be large, heterogeneous, and only partially useful for a target domain, while target labels are scarce.

The project combines multiple feature- and gradient-based valuation signals through surrogate-guided optimization to select compact training subsets under fixed training budgets. The goal is to reduce source-data usage while improving target-domain adaptation under label scarcity and distribution shift.

**Role:** First-author research project at USC.

**Stack:** PyTorch, MONAI, NumPy, scikit-learn, EfficientViT/nnU-Net validation, Linux/Slurm experiment pipelines.
