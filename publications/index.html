2026

  1. Under Review
    Target-Aware Adaptive Multi-Criteria Data Valuation for Budgeted Cross-Domain Medical Image Segmentation
    Xiwen Chen and Ruishan Liu
    Manuscript under review, 2026
    Abs Bib Website

    A target-aware data valuation framework for budgeted source curation under target-domain distribution shift. TAVO fuses multiple feature- and gradient-based valuation signals through surrogate-guided optimization to select compact training subsets under fixed training budgets.

  2. Under Review
    Target-Anchored Coverage for Annotation-Efficient Cross-Domain Medical Image Segmentation
    Xiwen Chen and Ruishan Liu
    Manuscript under review, 2026
    Abs Bib Website

    An annotation-efficient source-target curation method that couples active target acquisition with source selection. Queried target labels act as reliability anchors for constructing compact, target-aligned source subsets.
